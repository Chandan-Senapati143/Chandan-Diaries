# 📖 Chandan Diaries

> A personalized digital vault, daily routine tracker, academic archive, and utility studio designed for mobile and desktop.

![Chandan Diaries Preview](https://media.giphy.com/media/LmN8OYiY4m0X85K0Zz/giphy.gif)

---

## 🌟 Overview

**Chandan Diaries** is an all-in-one personal workspace built to organize daily productivity, store verified academic qualifications, manage family document vaults, and process application-ready photos and signatures on demand.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 📅 Daily Routine Tracker
* **Day & Time Customization:** Schedule tasks for specific days (e.g., *Sunday*) and exact time slots (e.g., *10:00 AM - 11:00 AM*).
* **Automatic Daily Reset:** Clears checkboxes at midnight while recording daily progress.
* **Consistency Analytics:** Visual percentage tracker showing today's completion and 7-day rolling performance averages.

### 2. 🎓 Academics Vault
A structured record of educational milestones:
* **Matriculation:** Jajpur Zilla High School, Jajpur (Passed: May 2015 | 78.84%)
* **Intermediate:** Vinayak College of Science & Commerce, Bhadrak (Stream: Science | Passed: May 2015 | 79.17%)
* **Graduation:** Government College of Engineering, Keonjhar (Course: B. Tech | Branch: Mechanical Engineering | Passed: July 2022 | 85.30% / 9.03 CGPA)
* **Post-Graduation:** Institute of Management & Information Technology, Cuttack (Course: MBA | Specialization: Finance & Operation Management | Passed: June 2025 | 78.00% / 8.3 CGPA)

### 3. 📁 Files & Family Drives
* **My Drive:** Dedicated sub-vaults for *Certificates*, *Mail IDs*, and *Other Documents*.
* **Family Drives:** Separate storage compartments for *Father's Drive* and *Mother's Drive*.
* **Direct Uploads:** Add files directly from any phone, tablet, or laptop.

### 4. 📷 Photo & Signature Studio
* **Dimension Precision:** Set dimensions in **Pixels (px)** or physical **Centimeters (cm)** with automatic 300 DPI calculations.
* **Custom Shapes:** Instant conversion between standard rectangle, circular avatar, and rounded corners.
* **Multi-Format Export:** Download processed assets directly as **JPG**, **PNG (transparent)**, or **PDF**.

### 5. 🎯 Goal
* A dedicated workspace initialized for tracking career milestones and long-term targets.

### 6. 🕒 Live Weather & Location Header
* Dynamic location detection with real-time digital clock and calendar integration.

## 🛠️ Built With

* **Frontend:** HTML5, Modern Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
* **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) & Custom Glassmorphism UI
* **Icons:** [FontAwesome 6](https://fontawesome.com/)
* **Document Engine:** [jsPDF](https://github.com/parallax/jsPDF)
* **Storage:** Browser `localStorage` (Client-side privacy & persistence)
